Traffic and Google Ads Fundamentals

Set up a Google Ads campaign for a naturopathic practice without wasting budget — covering account structure, keyword match types, negative keyword lists, landing page requirements, and Ontario-specific compliance considerations.

Paid search is a fast way to get in front of patients who are actively looking for a naturopathic doctor — but it's also a fast way to burn budget if the campaign is set up incorrectly. This checklist walks through the decisions that determine whether a Google Ads campaign is efficient and compliant, from campaign structure and match types through landing page requirements and regulatory constraints on health advertising in Ontario.

What this checklist covers

  • Campaign and ad group structure for a ND practice
  • Keyword match types — when to use phrase versus exact
  • Building a negative keyword list before spend starts
  • Location targeting for a single-clinic practice
  • Ad copy restrictions for healthcare in Google's policies
  • Ontario CONO advertising guidelines and compliance
  • Landing page requirements for quality score and conversions
  • Conversion tracking setup before campaign activation

Ads work better with a good website underneath them

The most common reason a Google Ads campaign underperforms for a naturopathic practice isn't the campaign — it's the landing page. Paid traffic amplifies whatever is already true about your website. If the site converts organic visitors, it will convert paid visitors. If it doesn't, no amount of campaign optimization will fix that. This checklist helps you set up ads correctly and ensure the website they're pointing to is ready to convert.
