Patient-Question Content Research

Use real patient language, search suggestions, first-party questions, Search Console, and research tools to find content topics that are worth writing about — not just topics that seem relevant.

The best content for a naturopathic practice website answers questions patients are already asking — in the language they actually use, not the clinical terminology that comes naturally to a practitioner. This checklist walks you through where to find those questions, how to evaluate whether a topic is worth writing about, and how to build a content list you can actually work through.

What this checklist covers

  • Collecting questions directly from patients in practice
  • Using Google autocomplete and People Also Ask
  • Reading Search Console for existing impressions data
  • Free and low-cost keyword research tools for NDs
  • Evaluating topic difficulty and search intent
  • Prioritizing topics by patient need versus SEO potential
  • Ontario regulatory considerations for health content topics
  • Building a content backlog you can actually work through

Start with the questions you already hear

The most effective starting point for content research is the questions patients ask you in appointments. These are real questions in real language, and they reflect genuine uncertainty or curiosity. Once you've captured those, this checklist shows you how to verify there's search demand for them and how to find related topics you might have missed.
